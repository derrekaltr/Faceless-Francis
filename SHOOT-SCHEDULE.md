# Director Video — Shoot Schedule

The 12 weekly video scripts batched into **3 studio days of 4 scripts each**, grouped by content arc and sequenced to stay ahead of the weekly drop calendar (`ROADMAP.md`). One presenter (ALTR social media director), one consistent set and look across all 12 so the series reads as a single program.

Scripts live at `weeks/week-NN/video-script.md`; teleprompter PDFs at `weeks/week-NN/ALTR-organic-growth-wkNN-video-script.pdf`.

**Shoot the program's own videos to the program's own standards:** Week 11 framing/lighting (eye-level, relatable-expert; key at 45° + bounce; expose for the face) and Week 12 audio (voice −6 to −3 dB, music −18 to −12 dB, 3–5 SFX/30s, captions mandatory, text inside center 80%).

## Standing setup — identical every shoot day

- **Set:** charcoal backdrop (`#1d1d1b`), mint accent (`#b6f2e5`) — matches the PDF brand.
- **Framing:** eye level, waist-up anchor; the Week 11 shot ladder for any cutaways.
- **Lighting:** 1-point key at 45° above eye line + hair light + bounce fill at 50–70%.
- **Audio:** lav mic; levels per Week 12 spec; triple-playback QC.
- **Teleprompter:** load the week's `...-video-script.pdf`; mint `[GFX]`/`[B-ROLL]` boxes are cues, NOT read aloud.
- **Wardrobe:** one consistent anchor look across all 12 for series cohesion (optionally rotate a single accent per batch; keep it subtle).
- **Lower-third:** "[NAME] · Social Media Director, ALTR" at 0:05 of each.
- **Per take:** hook delivered in first 3 seconds with motion in frame one; "next week" tease + ALTR end-card on every video except Week 12 (program finale — no tease).

## Post-production per batch (editor)

1. Pull the `[GFX:]` and `[B-ROLL:]` cues straight from each `weeks/week-NN/video-script.md`.
2. Cut to the program's own retention standard (Week 12): first-frame motion, re-hook ~every 3s, zero dead air, captions on inside center 80%, audio to spec.
3. Deliver each batch **before its first drop date** above, so there's buffer before the Monday it posts.
