Body

Here's the mistake: most creators treat their most noticeable physical trait as their niche. That worked years ago, when demand outran supply. Today every looks-based lane is packed — and AI-generated creators are about to flood them further. The one moat nobody can copy is a real, differentiated personality inside an interest the algorithm can actually target.

GFX: CATEGORY → NICHE → SUBNICHE pyramid

So think in three tiers. Category: fitness, gaming, cooking. Millions of creators. You cannot compete there — remember the matchmaker from week one? It can't match "cooking" to anybody. Niche: powerlifting. Southern cooking. Now the algorithm has a target. Subniche: women's beginner powerlifting. Louisiana Creole cooking. That's the sweet spot — specific enough that your audience looks at you and thinks, "she's one of us."

How do you know you've drilled deep enough? One test. Can you describe the one specific fan this serves — the person from last week, with a name, a job, and a reason he's scrolling at one in the morning? If your niche could describe half of Instagram, keep drilling.

Then we run the gut check. Three letters. A — could you talk about this for hours on camera? Passion reads. Boredom reads louder. T — market size. We want roughly five million people. The course this program is built on sized Mormon home cooking at three to seven million — big enough to build a business, small enough to be the queen of. And D — can you produce it every single day without burning out or going broke? A kitchen? Yes. Skydiving? Not unless you own a plane.

B-ROLL: scrolling a competitor's Reels grid, then the comment section

And before we commit, my team does one more thing. We pull the top creators in your subniche — around two hundred and fifty of their best Reels — and we don't copy them. We read their comments. Because comment sections tell you exactly what the audience wants that nobody is making. That gap is where you enter.

Homework + close

Your homework is one piece of paper: write your category, your niche, your subniche. Can't get past category? Then write the three things you could talk about for two hours straight — your niche is hiding in there, and we'll find it together.
