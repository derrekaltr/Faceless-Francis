## Script

**COLD OPEN (hook, ≤5s)**

A million views sounds amazing — until none of them click your link. Let me show you why that happens, and how to stop it.

**INTRO**

I'm [NAME], your social media director at ALTR — welcome to week seven. Today's trick is the one that separates creators who grow a number from creators who grow an income.

[GFX: "WK 07 — AUDIENCE QUALITY"]

**BODY**

Here's the idea. Imagine a billboard. One version is seen by ten thousand people who want exactly what you offer. The other is seen by a million random people. Which is worth more? The ten thousand — every time. Because your people buy, and a crowd just scrolls past.

So views are not the goal. The *right* views are.

And this leads somewhere uncomfortable: some growth is bad growth. There are tactics that pump up your numbers with people who will never support you — and they actually make things worse, because they teach the algorithm to go find *more* of the wrong people.

[GFX: three offenders appear]

Three big ones. Generic thirst-bait — it needs no shared language, so it attracts everyone and connects with no one. Shoutouts and follow-swaps — you inherit somebody else's mismatched audience. And chasing viral trends outside your niche — one wrong hit poisons your next several posts.

[B-ROLL: scrolling a comment section]

Now here's the empowering part: your content *is* your filter. Your humor, your references, the music, the inside jokes — every choice either pulls your people closer or waters your audience down. Content that your people need their own context to fully get? That screens out everyone else automatically. A video only your people love builds an account of only your people. That's not a limitation — that's the whole strategy.

**HOMEWORK + CLOSE**

So this week: take your three highest-view posts from the last month and look past the view count. Did your people react — saves, real comments, DMs — or did a crowd just pass through? Then kill one bad-growth habit. One shoutout swap, one generic trend, one thirst-bait post. Give that slot to something only your people would love.

[GFX: "HOMEWORK: did your PEOPLE react — or just a crowd?"]

Next week, we build the posting machine — how often to post, and the five stages of scaling without burning out. See you then.
