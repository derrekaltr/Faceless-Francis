# SOP — Monthly Audience-Quality Review (content-side)

> Program week: 07 · Owner: account manager (per creator) · Cadence: monthly · Status: draft
> Fills gap in `weeks/week-07/gap-audit.md`. Destination: `altr-management/03-content-ops/organic-growth-program/`.
> Scope: content-side audience FIT only. Audience geography/economics and account operations are out of scope (see ops appendix §3 and the internal account brief).

## Purpose

Confirm each creator's content is attracting the *right* audience — their defined target (Wk 3 persona, Wk 4 niche) — not just a large one, and catch "bad growth" before it dilutes the account and the algorithm's targeting.

## Trigger

Monthly, alongside the Wk 1 metrics grading. Also run after any unusually viral post to check for audience contamination.

## Procedure

1. Pull the top 5 posts by views for the month.
2. For each, compare reach against engagement depth (saves, comments, DM rate). High views + thin depth = crowd, not fit.
3. Sample 20–30 recent comments. Classify: in the audience's own language/context (fit) vs. generic (mismatch).
4. Check the follower-vs-link-click trend from the Wk 1 report. Followers up with clicks flat = growth without fit.
5. Flag any "bad growth" inputs in play: bought followers, shoutout/follow swaps, off-niche trend chasing. Recommend stopping them.
6. Record the month's read in the creator's `profile.md` (fit: strong / mixed / diluted) and set one content action.

## Thresholds & actions

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Top posts are high-view / low-depth | Shift next cycle toward context-dependent content; stop thirst-bait |
| Comments majority generic | Audience is diluting — tighten niche signals (Wk 4), recognizable elements (Wk 6) |
| Post-viral reach drop | Confirm Week 1 contamination pattern; return to core format for 1–2 weeks |
| Any bought-follower / swap activity | Stop immediately; note in account record |

## Tools

- Wk 1 metrics report · native insights · ReelCritic · creator `profile.md`.

## Escalation

Fit "diluted" two months running → content strategy review with the content lead (format/compass fit, Wk 5).
