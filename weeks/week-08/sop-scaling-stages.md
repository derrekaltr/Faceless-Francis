# SOP — Scaling Stages, Posting Cadence & Volume Rules

> Program week: 08 · Owner: content lead · Cadence: weekly stage review (same sitting as Wk 1 metrics grading) · Status: draft
> Fills gap in `weeks/week-08/gap-audit.md`. Destination: `altr-management/03-content-ops/organic-growth-program/`.
> Scope: content strategy and scheduling only — account-infrastructure practices (IP/proxy/warmup/health-kill mechanics) are out of bounds for this program per coordinator ruling.

## Purpose

Every promo account has a named scaling stage, a written gate to the next one, and posting settings nobody improvises. Volume only ever increases on evidence (the Wk 1 grades), never on enthusiasm or follower milestones — so quality always precedes quantity.

## Trigger

Weekly, alongside the Wk 1 metrics grading (`weeks/week-01/sop-metrics-reporting.md`). Ad-hoc when an account shows plateau signals (HPF trending to 0, VTFR declining on unchanged content) or when a stage advancement is proposed.

## Procedure

1. **Record the stage** — one row per account in the account tracker: Account · Platform set · Stage (1–5) · Current volume · Gate to next stage · Last reviewed.
2. **Enforce the fixed settings** on every account, every stage:
   - Posting slots **9a / 12p / 3p ET** (9 and 12 strongest); schedule into slots, no off-slot posting.
   - Uploads at **1080p, never 4K** (platform compression ruins 4K).
   - Quality bar **7/10 at 1–3 posts/day**; carousels **capped at 1/day — never expand past it**.
   - Cross-post every piece to every platform in the account set (optimize for the primary platform; the rest is free incremental exposure).
3. **Stage definitions & volume rules:**
   - **S1 Foundation** — one account per platform per creator; 7 Reels + 7 carousels/week optimized for the primary platform (Instagram by default), cross-posted everywhere.
   - **S2 Posting volume** — step Reels to 2/day, then 3/day, as quality holds. Carousels stay at 1/day. *Course caveat:* if platforms are currently punishing high volume on single accounts, skip S2 and go to S3.
   - **S3 Account volume** — at diminishing returns on the main account (typically 100K–500K followers depending on niche): launch a second account on a **new format** (not a clone). Launch play: batch one week of content before launch → 1 Reel/day → **collab-tag every post with the main account** → **daily story CTA on the main account** pointing to the new one (The Weekly Edit supplies the story slots). The course's case study reports ~90K organic followers in 3 weeks; treat as reported result, not a target commitment. Platforms treat the accounts as separate creators, so fans see the creator twice per session — content minutes accumulate roughly twice as fast.
   - **S4 Platform-specific** — native content per platform; only once the multi-account system runs with spare capacity, usually with platform specialists hired.
   - **S5 Full production** — complete creative team; founder-level planning, outside this SOP.
4. **Advancement gates (capacity + evidence, never follower counts):**
   - S1 → S2: 7+7/week sustained ≥ 4 consecutive weeks at the 7/10 bar, with grades healthy (VTFR ≥ 0.5, AWT ≥ 3s).
   - S2 → S3: 2–3 Reels/day sustained at quality AND plateau signals confirmed by the Wk 1 grades (not one bad week).
   - S3 → S4: multi-account output fully operationalized with documented spare production capacity; specialist hiring approved.
   - Record the gate evidence in the tracker row before changing a stage.
5. **Step-down rule** — if grades fall below healthy (VTFR < 0.5 or AWT < 3s) for 4 consecutive weeks after a volume increase, hold or step volume back one notch and run ReelCritic on the recent slate before trying again. Volume never outruns quality.
6. **Sawdust extraction** — at every shoot/edit handoff, log the derivative set before the primary cut ships: base Reel → variant hooks (Trial Reels pipeline), stills → carousel (within the 1/day cap), long cut → clips, caption → story text. Nothing goes to waste.
7. **Greatest Hits recycling** — any post that beat its account's follower count or cleared VTFR 1.0 goes on the Greatest Hits list; re-schedule each winner **~30 days after its last run** (full 5-folder system arrives in Wk 12 — keep a simple list until then).
8. **Audience steering (reporting language)** — in client reports, audience-composition work is described only as: "we steer your audience toward the fans most likely to support you." Operationally that means content signals — caption language, humor, references, music, setting — chosen for the creator's target audience. No other audience tactics are in scope for this program.

## Thresholds & kill criteria

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| 7+7/week at 7/10 quality, 4 straight weeks, grades healthy | Eligible S1 → S2 |
| Volume sustained but VTFR < 0.5 or AWT < 3s for 4 weeks | Step volume back; ReelCritic review before re-advancing |
| HPF → 0 trend + VTFR decline on unchanged content at 2–3 Reels/day | Plateau confirmed — propose S3 second-account launch |
| Carousel output proposed > 1/day | Reject — hard cap, no exceptions |
| Any post > follower count or VTFR ≥ 1.0 | Add to Greatest Hits; repost ~30 days later |
| Platforms punishing single-account volume (periodic) | Skip S2 → S3 per course note; document the call |

## Tools

- Account tracker sheet — stage, volume, gates, review dates.
- Metricool — slot scheduling (9a/12p/3p ET) and cross-posting.
- Wk 1 metrics SOP + ReelCritic — the evidence layer for every gate and step-down.
- Trial Reels pipeline — S1/S2 volume production; The Weekly Edit — S3 daily story CTA.
- Greatest Hits list (sheet until Wk 12's 5-folder system lands).

## Escalation

Every S3 second-account launch is a founder-level call (which creator, which format — ops appendix §4) → Derrek before launch. Stage-advancement disputes between content lead and account manager → Derrek with the tracker row and 4-week grade history attached.
