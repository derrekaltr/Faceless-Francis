Posting cadence & the five stages every account climbs

When your posts go out, how much to post at every level, and how one account becomes an ecosystem — without ever letting quality slip.

The idea

The number one scaling mistake in this space is growing presence before quality. The most efficient factory in the world doesn't matter if what it makes isn't worth buying. So we scale like you'd set fishing lines: one line in the water, properly set, before the next goes in.

Three concepts drive everything. Exposure — how often a potential fan sees your content. Content minutes — a fan needs roughly 15–20 minutes of accumulated watch time before they trust you enough to pay; every post is minutes in the bank. And the ecosystem — if someone sees you every time they open Instagram, that's good. If they see you on Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube, the minutes pile up several times faster. That's why everything we make gets cross-posted everywhere: free extra reach for zero extra creative effort.

Two force multipliers as you grow: use your sawdust — every shoot is raw material for more content (Reels, carousels, clips — like a lumber mill, nothing goes to waste) — and repost your Greatest Hits roughly every 30 days. Proven winners are free volume most creators never collect.

This week's play Fill all three posting slots on schedule, every day, at your current stage's volume — and never add volume until the current level is steady at quality. More, posted worse, is less.

The machine's settings (we don't improvise these)

9a·12p·3pET posting slots — 9 and 12 are the strongest
1080pupload resolution — platforms compress 4K badly
15–20 minwatch time a fan needs before they're ready to pay

Output holds a 7/10-quality bar at 1–3 posts a day, carousels cap at one a day, and your weekly grades — watch time, view-to-follower ratio, high-performance frequency — decide when volume climbs. The same content signals that win your niche (language, humor, music, setting) are how we steer your audience toward the fans most likely to support you.

How accounts scale — the five stages

StageWhat happens
1 · FoundationOne account per platform. 7 Reels + 7 carousels a week, built for your main platform, cross-posted everywhere.
2 · Posting volume2–3 Reels a day once quality holds. Carousels stay at one a day — forever.
3 · Account volumeMain account plateaus → we launch a second account with a new format, collab-tag every post with the main, and run a daily story pointing fans to it. The course's case study took a brand-new account to ~90K followers in 3 weeks this way.
4 · Platform-specificContent made natively for each platform, not just cross-posted. Years in; specialists hired.
5 · Full productionA complete creative team — where the biggest creators in the world operate.
"The stage you're at depends on production capacity, not a follower count. Quality before quantity — scaling a weak formula just manufactures more of what doesn't work."

Do / don't

Do

  • Cross-post everything — free reach on every other platform.
  • Squeeze derivatives out of every shoot, and repost proven winners ~every 30 days.
  • Let your weekly grades decide when volume climbs.

Don't

  • Post past one carousel a day — there's no benefit beyond it.
  • Upload 4K. Crisp out of the editor, mush after compression.
  • Jump a scaling stage because a follower milestone feels close.

This week's homework

  1. Check last week's posts against the 9a / 12p / 3p schedule — flag anything that went out off-slot.
  2. Ask your manager which scaling stage your account is in, and exactly what the gate to the next one is.
  3. Find your best post from more than 30 days ago — that's your first Greatest Hit going back into the schedule.
