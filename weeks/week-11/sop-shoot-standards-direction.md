# SOP — Shoot Standards & Creator Direction

> Program week: 11 · Owner: account manager (direction + comms) / creative lead (frame + light QC) · Cadence: every shoot day + weekly content-delivery review · Status: draft
> Fills gap in `weeks/week-11/gap-audit.md`. Destination: `altr-management/03-content-ops/organic-growth-program/`.

## Purpose

Every clip entering the pipeline meets one visual standard — deliberate shot, deliberate angle, correctly exposed, believably lit — and every instruction to a creator is specific, data-backed, and respectful. Footage quality and creator retention are both communication outcomes; this SOP covers both ends of the camera.

## Trigger

Before and during every directed shoot; weekly when self-shot content is delivered; at signing for every new creator (§C deliverables clause).

## Procedure

### A. Pre-shoot (shot list + setup)

1. Build the shot list from the creator's content bank (Wk 10 SOP) — per clip: **shot type · angle · intent**. Shot distance = importance (close = emotion/intimacy, waist-up = hands/body language, full/wide = outfit or environment). Angle = status (slightly below = aspirational/in charge · above = soft/vulnerable · eye level = relatable peer). Default to eye level unless the intent says otherwise.
2. Background check: the set must communicate the persona without words (Wk 6 brand kit). Remove off-brand elements before the first take.
3. Light — pick the rung, don't improvise:
   - **One light**: key at 45° to one side, slightly above eye level, angled down. Kill competing overheads. White bounce board (~$10) opposite as fill; a window at 45° works as a free key (warm at morning/evening, harsh and cool midday).
   - **+ Hair light**: behind and above the subject, aimed at hair/shoulders, never flaring the lens. First upgrade to buy.
   - **3-point**: add fill opposite the key at 50–70% of key intensity. If both sides of the face read the same brightness, the fill is too hot.
   - Motivation check: light direction and warmth must match the visible source (window, lamp). Matched color temperature across all lights, or mismatch only on purpose.
4. Camera: **expose for the face** · ISO at/near base, brightness from lights not gain · shutter = 1/(2× framerate), then leave it · phone shoots use locked manual exposure (Moment app, ~$10) — never full auto · low f-stop for subject-pop, high f-stop when the background must read.

### B. On set (direction)

5. Frame check before every setup, out loud, 30 seconds: *shot? angle? light motivated? face exposed?*
6. Direction is specific and actionable — "chin toward the light", "slow the turn", "once more with the laugh" — never "do it better" or "again". If words fail, demonstrate (a filmed reference take is worth keeping).
7. Direction stays inside the creator's contracted comfort levels (per `onlyfans-content-script-gpt` content tiers). Pushing past them on set is prohibited — misalignment pauses the shoot and goes to Derrek.
8. Reshoot triggers: see thresholds table. Catch on set; a reshoot costs minutes, a bad batch costs a week.

### C. Creator communication kit (account managers)

9. **Contract deliverables clause** — set at signing, numbers per creator, never vague. Template: "[15] raw video clips and [10] photo sets per week; videos 30–90 seconds; photo sets 8–12 images minimum; delivered by Sunday midnight for the following week's posting schedule." Enforcement ladder: documented warning → contractual fee → termination review.
10. **Expectation setting — honesty rule**: project only from results actually delivered with similar creators ("based on similar creators, we anticipate [5,000–10,000] followers and [$10–20K] monthly within 8–12 weeks; we review progress biweekly and adjust"). Never guarantee a floor. Trust compounds; an overpromise never does.
11. **Feedback framework** — every weekly delivery and every reshoot request:
    - Never send feedback while frustrated. Write it, delete it, come back in an hour.
    - Open with something true and positive (on time, best clip, improvement).
    - Notes are specific and actionable ("the bedroom clips are dark — shoot near the window or add the light") — never "the lighting is bad".
    - Explain **why** with performance data ("your smiling clips consistently outperform — that's why I'm asking").
    - Ask for the fix; don't order it.
    - Pick your battles: 13 excellent clips beat 15 mediocre ones. Spend corrective energy only on what moves the numbers.
12. Log delivery hit rate and feedback sent in the creator's `profile.md`; fold into the monthly review alongside the Wk 2 stage diagnostic.

## Thresholds & kill criteria

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Blown highlights or crushed blacks on the face | Reshoot — non-negotiable |
| Light fights the visible source (bad motivation) / mixed color temps | Fix before the next setup; flag for the editor if it slipped through |
| Key dead-center or fill at key brightness | Re-rig before rolling — flat footage is a silent VTFR tax |
| Deliverables missed 2 weeks running | Warning per ladder + a call to diagnose (don't assume the cause) |
| Missed after fees applied / 3 cycles | Termination review with Derrek |
| Feedback drafted while frustrated | Not sent — one-hour rule, no exceptions |

## Tools

- Shot-list sheet (per shoot: shot · angle · intent · setup rung) — lives with the content bank.
- ~$10 bounce board / white poster board · consumer LED lights (3000–6000K covers nearly everything).
- Moment app (~$10) — iPhone manual exposure lock.
- `content-drop-automation` — shoot-idea intake feeding shot lists · creator `profile.md` — feedback + delivery log.

## Escalation

Chronic non-delivery after the enforcement ladder, any comfort-level conflict on set, or a feedback exchange going sideways (creator disengaging, emotional escalation) → stop, escalate to Derrek with the log. Never resolved by pressure on set or in DMs.
