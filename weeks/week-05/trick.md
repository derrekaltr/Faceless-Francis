# WK 05 — The 70/20/10 Content Portfolio + the Content Compass Lock

> Drop date: Dec 21, 2026 · Source lessons: 2.7 (content formats), 2.10 (Content Compass) · KB 2.4 / 2.7

## The promise (one sentence, client-facing)

Stop reinventing your content every morning: build a portfolio of repeatable formats around one proven winner, and lock every post into the one position on the compass that your fans pay for.

## The idea

Quality content at daily volume is impossible if every post starts from a blank page. The answer is **formats** — repeatable, recognizable templates you reuse, where the structure stays the same and only the content inside changes. A good format passes three tests: **legibility** (does your audience instantly get what it is and why it's for them?), **replicability** (could you make this every day for months without getting sick of it?), and **practicality** (can you afford it in time, money, location, and energy?). A format that needs a new set, a costume, or a shopping trip per video fails before the algorithm ever sees it.

Formats are found by testing, not taste. Post a candidate format daily and within about two weeks you have a clear picture of winners and losers. Then get analytical about the winners: isolate the elements that might be driving them — hook, topic, outfit, setting — and recreate them with slight variations. A format that consistently grades around **0.5 VTFR becomes your core format — and once you have one, you ride it until it dies.** One humbling pattern the course reports from its own numbers: effort doesn't predict performance. Polished passion projects routinely get beaten by simple text-over-B-roll — which is exactly why you test instead of guessing.

From there, every account runs a portfolio: **70% core format** (your proven winner), **20% batchable filler** (simple, easy to produce in batches — text over B-roll, trends adapted into your niche), **10% experiments** (ideas with viral potential; most fail, but one becomes your next core format). All three buckets stay 100% inside your niche — a trend is a delivery vehicle, never a detour. The move is *making the universal feel specific*: take a trend everyone recognizes and filter it through your niche's inside jokes, so your audience feels it was made for them while the algorithm still recognizes the trend.

Formats answer *what you make*. The **Content Compass** answers *how it lands*. X-axis: **value** (educate, instruct, inform) vs. **entertainment** (make them feel something — both must be engaging). Y-axis: **attainable** (creator on the viewer's level or below — flawed, relatable, "one of us"; buying runs on **hope** of connection) vs. **aspirational** (creator above the viewer — authority, glamour, spectacle; buying runs on **admiration and devotion**). A hidden Z-axis — the specific emotion you evoke — is what keeps two creators in the same quadrant from feeling identical. No quadrant is better; both a warm "I've been where you are" coach and a drill-sergeant authority outperform the generic middle, because both are differentiated.

## This week's trick (the single imperative)

Pick your one compass quadrant with your manager and lock it — then every post this week comes from a named bucket (core / filler / experiment) *and* lands in that quadrant. No posts from "nowhere".

## Do / don't

**Do:** keep the structure constant and vary the content inside it · get forensic about *why* winners won (hook? topic? setting?) and iterate on those elements · adapt trends into your niche as inside jokes · commit to one quadrant and go deeper via the Z-axis emotion.
**Don't:** post mostly experiments to "keep things fresh" — fresh is what the 10% is for; the 70% is what grows you · drop the core format because *you're* bored of it — retire it on the numbers, not the mood · hop quadrants post to post — the undifferentiated middle converts nobody · copy a format your audience won't instantly understand, however well it performs elsewhere.

## This week's homework (client actions)

1. List every format you currently post. Score each 1–5 on legibility, replicability, practicality — anything under 10/15 is a filler candidate at best.
2. With your ALTR manager: pull VTFR history per format. Is there a core-format candidate at ~0.5? If yes, name it. If not, pick the strongest and test it daily for two weeks.
3. Sort your last 20 posts onto the compass. If they don't cluster in one quadrant, choose the quadrant that fits you best — and commit in writing.
