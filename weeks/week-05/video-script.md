## Script

**COLD OPEN (hook, ≤5s)**

The post you spent all day on just lost to a lazy clip with text on it. That's not bad luck — that's the lesson.

**INTRO**

I'm [NAME], your social media director at ALTR — week five. You know your numbers, your fans, your one specific person, your niche. This week: what you actually post every day — without burning out or guessing.

[GFX: "WK 05 — THE 70/20/10 PORTFOLIO"]

**BODY**

Here's the trap: starting every post from a blank page. The creators who win don't. They run formats — repeatable templates where the structure stays the same and only the content inside changes. A good format passes three tests. Does your audience instantly get it? Could you make it every single day without getting sick of it? And can you afford it — in time, money, and energy?

[GFX: "LEGIBLE · REPLICABLE · PRACTICAL"]

And you don't pick formats on taste. You test. Post a candidate daily, and in about two weeks the numbers give you a verdict. Remember effort doesn't decide this — simple beats polished all the time. When a format consistently pulls about half as many views as you have followers, that's your core format. And once you find it? You ride it until it dies. You don't drop it because you got bored. You drop it when the numbers say so.

[GFX: 70 / 20 / 10 split bar]

From there, your week is a portfolio. Seventy percent core format — your proven winner. Twenty percent filler — easy stuff you batch, like text over B-roll and trends adapted into your niche. Ten percent experiments — wild ideas, most fail, and one becomes your next core. And all of it — all of it — stays in your niche. A trend is a delivery vehicle. You filter it through your world until it feels like an inside joke for your fans.

[B-ROLL: compass graphic building — two axes, four quadrants]

One more thing, and it's the big one. Every post sits somewhere on a compass. Are you giving value or entertainment? And are you attainable — on your audience's level, one of them — or aspirational — above them, someone they look up to? Attainable fans buy on hope. Aspirational fans buy on admiration. Both work. What doesn't work is the middle. Relatable on Monday, untouchable on Tuesday — that reads as nobody, and nobody converts. So we pick your one quadrant together, write it down, and every post hits it. Every single one.

**HOMEWORK + CLOSE**

Your homework — one thing: take your last twenty posts and sort them. Core, filler, or experiment — and mark whether each one lands in your quadrant. Most creators find out they're running ninety percent experiments, scattered all over the compass. Now you'll know — and now we can fix it.

[GFX: "HOMEWORK: sort your last 20 posts"]

Next week: the brand kit — your look, your sound, and the one-second test. How a stranger knows it's you before you've said a word. See you then.
