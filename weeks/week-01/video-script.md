# WK 01 Video Script — The Algorithm Is a Matchmaker

> Presenter: ALTR social media director · Audience: ALTR client (creator) watching
> Runtime target: ~3 min (≈430 words of dialogue at ~140 wpm)
> Setting: clean branded set (charcoal backdrop, mint accent) · Framing: eye level (relatable-expert)

## Script

**COLD OPEN (hook, ≤5s)**

Your last post got a few thousand views — a fraction of what you usually pull. And no, you're not shadowbanned. The algorithm ran a test on you. Your post failed it.

**INTRO**

Hey — I'm [NAME], your social media director here at ALTR. Every week I'm going to hand you one trick that changes how your content performs. This week: how the algorithm actually works — because once you see it, you'll never post the same way again.

[GFX: "WK 01 — THE ALGORITHM IS A MATCHMAKER"]

**BODY**

Here's the whole secret: the algorithm is a matchmaker. The platform makes money when people keep scrolling, so its only job is matching every viewer with content they'll actually watch. Help it make good matches, and it will put your content in front of your exact future fans — for free.

So what happens when you post?

[GFX: fingerprint → test group → boost ladder]

First, the platform fingerprints your video — what's in the frame, what you're saying, what your caption says. Then it runs a test. It shows your post to a small group — roughly two hundred people — and here's the part nobody tells you: most of them are strangers, not your followers. It wants to know if strangers stop scrolling for you.

If they do — boost. Two thousand people. Twenty thousand. Two hundred thousand. If they don't, it quietly stops.

That's what a post stuck way under your normal reach really is. Not a shadowban. A failed test. And the fix is not posting more, and it's not jumping on trends. It's making your content more specific — same topic, same audience, every single post — so the matchmaker knows exactly who you belong to.

Which also means one viral post outside your niche is a trap. It confuses your test groups, and your next several posts pay the price. The discipline is saying no.

[B-ROLL: creator insights screen, watch-time graph]

Now — how do we know it's working? At ALTR we grade your account on three numbers. Watch time: are people staying past three seconds — and how close are we to ten? Your view-to-follower ratio: a healthy account averages at least half as many views as it has followers, every post. And how often a post beats your follower count — because that's the number that actually grows you. You'll see all three in your weekly report from now on, with a grade.

**HOMEWORK + CLOSE**

This week, one thing: open your insights and find the post from last month people watched longest. Don't overthink why. That's your audience telling you what they want — and this week, you and I are making more of it.

[GFX: "HOMEWORK: find your longest-watched post"]

See you next week — we're talking about how a stranger becomes a fan who pays.
