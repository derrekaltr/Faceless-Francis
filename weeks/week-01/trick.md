# WK 01 — The Algorithm Is a Matchmaker (and the Only 3 Numbers That Matter)

> Drop date: Nov 23, 2026 · Source lessons: 1.2 (organic marketing), 1.3 (algorithm), 3.4 (metrics) · KB 1.2 / 1.3 / 3.2e

## The promise (one sentence, client-facing)

Understand how the algorithm actually decides who sees your content — and the three numbers ALTR tracks to grow your account on purpose instead of by luck.

## The idea

Social platforms make money by keeping people scrolling, so the algorithm has one job: match every viewer with content they'll keep watching. It's a matchmaker. Help it make good matches and it distributes your content to your exact future fans, for free.

When you post, the platform fingerprints the video (what it sees, what it hears, your caption) and builds a "fit score" — a prediction of who will like it. It then shows the post to a small test group of roughly 200 people, **mostly strangers, not followers**. Strong reaction → it boosts to ~2,000, then ~20,000, then ~200,000. Lukewarm → it re-tests with a new group. Weak → it stops.

That's why posts stall way under an account's normal reach (a few thousand views for an established name). It's almost never a shadowban — the content failed its test group. The fix is not posting more or chasing trends; it's making more specific content for a more specific audience, so the fit score sharpens. The algorithm structurally rewards niching down. And it's why one off-niche viral post is a trap: it contaminates your test groups, and your next several posts suffer for it.

## This week's play (the single imperative)

For the next two weeks, every single post serves the same narrow audience — no exceptions, no off-niche trends — while we grade your account on the three numbers below.

## Numbers that matter

| Metric | What it tells you | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **AWT** — average watch time | Is the content good? | Platform average ~3s · gold standard ~10s |
| **VTFR** — avg views per post ÷ followers (30-day) | Is the audience matched? | 0.5–0.7 healthy · 1.0+ excellent · 2.0+ niche domination · below 0.5 = problem |
| **HPF** — % of posts with views > followers | Are you still growing? | 0 for 30+ days = strategy is stale, change formats/hooks |

VTFR kills vanity-metric thinking: 100K followers averaging 100K views is excellent; 5M followers averaging 100K views is life support. (A famous 8.6M-follower creator grades ~0.18.) AWT keeps the fans you have; HPF is what grows the account.

## Do / don't

**Do:** post 1–3×/day (each post is another chance to appear in the same viewer's session) · prompt comments with a hard stance or a question your niche has opinions about (keyworded comments help the algorithm tag you) · adapt trends *into* your niche.
**Don't:** buy followers (fake followers poison every future test group) · jump on off-niche trends, however viral · obsess over posting time, hashtags, or captions — they're real but marginal ("window dressing").
