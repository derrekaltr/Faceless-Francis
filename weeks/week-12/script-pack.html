Now, the edit itself. The algorithm pays for one thing: retention — how many viewers make it to the end. So every clip passes a ten-point checklist. Motion in the very first frame, because a still frame gets swiped. A new reason to keep watching every three seconds. Every pause and every breath cut — that's how three minutes becomes forty-five seconds. Little zooms that make one phone look like a two-camera shoot. Captions always on, in your brand style, because most people watch muted. Every graphic earns its place and lands with a sound. And before anything ships, we listen three times: headphones, speakers, phone.

One more number. We cap most edits at an hour — because a strong seven-out-of-ten posted every day beats a perfect ten posted once a week. Every time.
