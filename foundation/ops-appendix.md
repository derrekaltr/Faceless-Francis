# Ops Appendix — Internal-Only Material (not weekly drops)

Course material that matters to ALTR as a company but should **not** go into client-facing weekly drops. Sources: lessons 3.7/3.8/3.10/3.B, 1.BONUS, live sessions, TEAM PLAYBOOK items 16–18 + policy decisions.

## 1. Creative team building & incentives (lessons 3.7–3.8, Live Session 3)

- **Delegation trigger:** recurring task, 5+ hrs/week, someone else at 70%+ quality → delegate. "Most people hire too late."
- **Hiring order:** editor → scheduling VA → brand manager.
- **Editor test before pay:** send 2–3 raw clips — "pick one, turn it into content using your creative judgment." Best editor = fewest notes. Hire for taste.
- **Rates:** local senior editor $55–80K/yr (LinkedIn ad requiring portfolio) · international dedicated editor via Clipt $3K/mo (then negotiate direct ~$1.5K/mo) · scheduling VA $2–5/hr.
- **Retention:** senior creative/brand manager gets **commission on model revenue** — explicitly so they don't defect to start their own agency.
- **Org design at scale:** platform ownership (head of IG / YT / X) instead of per-model managers — builds SMEs, breeds leaders, strengthens the model-recruiting pitch.
- **Culture:** share revenue data with editors ("they stop thinking about likes and start thinking about conversions"); zero-cost failure — the algorithm rewards novelty.

## 3. Demographics economics (lesson 3.B, Live Sessions 2.2, 4)

- Top 5 countries ≈ half of OF revenue; US user ≈ $7.59/person-yr vs India $0.09 — **85:1 LTV**. An all-Tier-3 audience needs 85x followers AND 85x chatter conversations for the same revenue (chatter-payroll lever).
- **Audit threshold: <60% Tier-1 → intervene.** Levers: consistent Tier-1 IP via scheduler (never VPN — flagged; schedulers whitelisted) + cultural anti-legibility (English-dependent humor, US references, country music pulls US / Brazilian funk pulls LatAm).
- Badly Tier-3-skewed account: **don't fix — launch new + funnel traffic.** Thirst traps, paid ads, SFS all drift Tier-3.

## 4. Strategic bets (founder decisions)

- **YouTube** (Live Sessions 1, 2.3): "highest-leverage, most underexplored OF marketing medium" — wealthier male demo, ~¼ the traffic of FB/IG but better conversion; insulated from AI-slop flood. All-or-nothing: real production + a model who wants to be a YouTuber. **Active crackdown: try-on hauls now get bans.** Method that worked (Kira, 520K subs): 20–25 completely different weekly videos to find the format, then milk it. Reddit: full-time specialist or ignore.
- **IG collab second-account play** (Wk 8 PDF covers the tactic; the decision of which model gets it is founder-level). Case: 500K plateaued skit account → try-on-haul second account, every post collab-tagged + daily story CTA → 90K organic in 3 weeks.
- **AI content/ops layer**: Brand Blueprint → LLM persistent memory per model ("write in Kira's voice"); Higgsfield/Kling for B-roll; competitor-transcription sweeps. Never for personality, edge, emotional timing, slang, comedy.

## 5. Chatter bridge (content ↔ chat cohesion)

Course thesis: *"the highest-LTV version of OnlyFans is a cohesive customer experience that starts with the marketing and ends with the chat team."*
- Archetype language sheets per model (Wk 3 produces these) — per-archetype buyer psychographics: Therapist = top spenders (high-stress men, no confidant, kink shame); Forbidden Fruit = top monetization; Damsel pairs with findom; Dominant targets overwhelmed decision-makers.
- Content Compass attainable-vs-aspirational script pairs double as chatter tone templates (Wk 5).
- Pre-scripted persona practice scenarios (flirty DM, hate comment, big win) so the model performs the character on command.
- Industry intel: "Chat Stars" chatting course coming (#4 on Infloww, ~$9M/mo chatting agency); Hive Media benchmark (60+ models, $1M+/mo, 300-device farm, 12K accounts).
